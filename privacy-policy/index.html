Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information HexRoulette may collect and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, HexRoulette may process:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server/Guild IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs
  • Discord role or permission information
  • Commands and interactions sent to the Bot
  • Configuration settings for individual servers

HexRoulette does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, or Discord account tokens.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide HexRoulette's features
  • Save server configuration
  • Respond to commands and interactions
  • Prevent abuse
  • Troubleshoot errors
  • Improve HexRoulette

3. Data Storage

Data is stored only when necessary for HexRoulette's functionality.

We do not permanently store message content.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services required to operate HexRoulette, such as Discord or hosting/database providers.

5. Data Retention

We retain stored information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide HexRoulette's services or comply with legal obligations.

You may request deletion of information associated with your server or account by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

HexRoulette is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when HexRoulette's functionality or data practices change.
